Selection rule

For each day:

-> Keep options with valid Close
-> Keep 20–40 days to expiry
-> Find the strike closest to that day's actual Bank Nifty close
-> If multiple contracts have the same/very similar ATM strike, choose expiry closest to 30 days
-> Use OI only as a tie-breaker, because it is frequently missing

We are not going to arbitrarily pick a fixed strike or fixed expiry.
